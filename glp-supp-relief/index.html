Why It Happens

Here’s Why GLP-1 Brings Your Digestion To A Crawl

GLP-1 medications work partly by slowing how fast your stomach empties. That’s what keeps you full for hours. But the same slowdown is why food sits, why you feel bloated after a few bites, and why a lot of people stop going regularly.

Laxatives and fiber gummies just push harder on a system that’s already strained. They don’t rebuild what’s actually missing.

The fix isn’t forcing things through. It’s rebuilding the gut bacteria the medication leaves behind, and feeding them so they hold.*
