The product, in one paragraph

CoreAge Rx GLP-1 Balance is a 30-capsule, once-daily prebiotic and probiotic blend marketed as a companion to GLP-1 therapy. It contains Chicory Root Inulin (211 mg) and Potato Resistant Starch (100 mg) as gut-feeding fiber, plus a 36 mg probiotic blend featuring Akkermansia muciniphila, Bifidobacterium infantis, and Clostridium butyricum. Label positioning: "Helps maintain balanced blood sugar levels as part of a healthy lifestyle." Not a weight-loss claim. Same competitive lane as glp1sos.com, but with the Akkermansia hero strain doing the heavy lifting on credibility.

  • 30 capsules per bottle, 1 daily, refrigerate
  • 3 hero strains, Akkermansia is the differentiator
  • Bills as gut and blood sugar support, not as a drug
  • FDA disclaimer present on the label

Primary persona, deepened  Phase 1, hottest

Linda, 54

Suburban Texas, white, married, two adult kids out of the house. Office manager at a regional insurance brokerage, makes around $78k, husband makes more. She has been on Ozempic for fourteen weeks. She has lost 23 pounds. She is also miserable in ways she did not expect.

Why she is the primary target. Solution-aware, product-aware, in active pain on a list of symptoms she can name. She is not deciding whether GLP-1 is for her, she is desperate to keep losing weight without feeling like her body is breaking. Hottest possible audience for a companion supplement. Lowest CPA window we will ever see.

Schwartz awareness state

DimensionState
Problem-awareFully. She can name every symptom and rank them.
Solution-awarePartial. She has read "take probiotics" and "drink electrolytes" but does not yet believe a specific supplement solves it.
Product-awareLow. She has heard "Akkermansia" in a podcast or TikTok but does not connect it to a buyable product.
Most awareNot yet. Reaching this state is what month 1 of ads is for.

Sophistication state of her ad feed

Her feed already contains compounded GLP-1 sellers, "natural Ozempic alternative" gummies, generic probiotic ads (Seed, Align, Ritual), and weight-loss coaching offers. She has seen 50+ supplement claims this year. We are entering an already-sophisticated market. Schwartz level 4 to 5. Headlines must lead with a unique mechanism (Akkermansia, the strain by name) or with identity (the patient your doctor never warned). Plain "supports gut health" is dead on arrival.

Buying triggers (the moments she pulls out a credit card)

  • Symptom spike. Day 3 of constipation, second meeting walked out of because of burps, a single morning of looking pregnant in the mirror.
  • Dose escalation week. 0.5mg to 1.0mg jump on Ozempic. New nausea wave, she goes looking for a fix that night.
  • Friend mentions Akkermansia. A peer in her FB group drops the strain name. She Googles. Our retargeting catches her.
  • Negative scale day. Stalled weight or first regain pound. She blames the gut, looks for "what to add."
  • Doctor invalidates her. Visit where her PCP shrugs at the side effects. She walks out angry. The doctor-villain frame lands hottest in this 48-hour window.

Awareness state ladder, week by week

The same audience reads our ads differently as they see more of them. Cycle the message accordingly. This is what kills accounts that "found a winner" and then watched CPM double in week 4.

Weeks 1 to 2 — Recognition

She has never seen our brand. Job of the ad: make her feel seen on the symptom. Headlines name the pain in her exact words, no product yet.

Example: "Day 5 and you still haven't gone? Here's why GLP-1 wrecks your gut."

Weeks 3 to 4 — Mechanism

She has seen us. Now introduce Akkermansia by name. Job: install a new buying criterion in her head ("I need the strain, not just any probiotic").

Example: "The bacteria missing in 90% of Ozempic users' guts."

Weeks 5 to 6 — Identity

She knows the strain. Now sell her the identity of the informed patient. Job: make taking CoreAge feel like belonging to the smart Ozempic users, not the suffering ones.

Example: "The 1 capsule the smartest Ozempic patients are taking quietly."

Weeks 7 to 8 — Risk reversal and proof

She has clicked but not bought. Job: hand her the trust scaffold. Testimonials, third-party COA, 30-day guarantee, refrigeration as proof of potency.

Example: "30 days. If your mornings don't change, send it back."

Sophistication ladder, with production-ready headlines

Schwartz 5 levels applied. The market is already at level 4 to 5. We lead there, hold a level 3 inventory for fatigue swaps, and pre-build levels 1 and 2 for retargeting and warm audiences only.

Lv 1 Direct claim (only useful as a retargeting refresher to people who already trust us)

  • Stop the sulphur burps.
  • 1 capsule a day. Gut back online.
  • Refrigerated probiotic for Ozempic users.

Lv 2 Bigger claim (dead in cold, alive in warm)

  • The fastest way to stop GLP-1 constipation, by morning.
  • End sulphur burps in 1 night, without quitting Ozempic.
  • Get your mornings back in under a week.

Lv 3 Mechanism (the new "how it works", first-time buyers' favorite)

  • The Akkermansia Protocol: 1 strain, 1 capsule, gut back in 7 days.
  • Why Ozempic wrecks your gut (and the 36mg fix doctors don't mention).
  • The bacteria scientists call "the Ozempic of the gut" is now in 1 capsule.
  • Press this strain into your gut tonight. Burps gone by morning.

Lv 4 New mechanism, our recommended cold-traffic lead (most of week 1 spend)

  • The Hidden Enemy of Ozempic Patients? This Bedtime Capsule Stops the Sulphur Burps.
  • The Greatest Enemy of Your Ozempic Results? This 1-Capsule Routine Restores the Strain Your Gut Lost.
  • The Real Reason GLP-1 Wrecks Your Gut? It Kills One Bacteria. We Put It Back.
  • Doctors Won't Tell Ozempic Patients About This Gut Bacteria.
  • What Endocrinologists Are Missing About GLP-1 Constipation.

Lv 5 Identity and us-vs-them (week 5+ for fatigue swap, or for the "smart patient" angle)

  • The Ozempic patients who don't suffer aren't lucky. They take this.
  • There are two kinds of Ozempic users. The ones who suffer, and the ones who know about Akkermansia.
  • The 1 capsule the smartest Ozempic patients are taking quietly.
  • The reason your sister-in-law's GLP-1 journey is easier than yours.

VSL angles, 3 distinct concepts  Cold-traffic conversion ranked

All 14 ads above feed one VSL, same as TinnitusWave's 17 ads feeding one. The three concepts below are alternative VSL leads to test against each other in month 2 once we have a hook winner from the static ads. Each one is internally consistent in lead, big idea, and pacing.

Rank 1 The Doctor Cover-Up (highest cold-traffic conversion bet)

Lead, first 30 seconds: "If you're on Ozempic, Mounjaro, or Wegovy and your gut feels like it's falling apart, your doctor is not going to tell you what I'm about to tell you. Not because they're hiding it. Because they don't know."

Big idea: GLP-1 drugs work by slowing the gut, and that slowing kills off one specific strain of bacteria, Akkermansia muciniphila, the same strain published research has been calling "the Ozempic of the gut." Without it, every symptom Linda has gets worse. With it back, the symptoms reverse.

Pacing: 9 to 11 minutes. Doctor villain framing in minute 1. Akkermansia introduced minute 2. Mechanism explained minutes 3 to 5. Testimonials minutes 5 to 7. Offer reveal minute 8. Risk reversal minute 9.

Why it ranks first: Pattern matches Linda's pre-existing resentment at her PCP, hits TinnitusWave mechanic 3 (authority dismissal) at maximum strength, and the Akkermansia strain provides the "new mechanism" that satisfies a level-4 sophistication market. Pirated framing of a proven funnel pattern.

Pills: Doctor villain New mechanism Lv 4 sophistication 9 min

Rank 2 The Bedtime Demonstration (closest to TinnitusWave's exact playbook)

Lead, first 30 seconds: "Tonight, before you go to bed, I'm going to show you a 9-second routine that has helped thousands of Ozempic patients wake up without the sulphur burps, without the bloating, and finally have a normal bathroom morning."

Big idea: The "routine" is taking one CoreAge capsule before bed. The 9-second framing borrows the TinnitusWave gesture-mechanic trick but adapted to ingestion (mechanic 2b). The transformation happens overnight, "by morning" is the emotional anchor that hits Linda's life metric directly.

Pacing: 7 to 9 minutes. Shorter than rank 1. Opens with bedtime demonstration, dramatizes the morning after, then backfills the science behind why it works.

Why it ranks second: Closest 1-to-1 transfer of the proven TinnitusWave funnel pattern, which is the highest-confidence template we have. Slightly behind rank 1 because it leads with promise rather than with the doctor-villain emotional charge, which underuses Linda's strongest pre-existing emotion.

Pills: Bedtime ritual By morning anchor Lv 3 to 4 7 min

Rank 3 The Smart Patient Identity (for retargeting and lookalikes, not pure cold)

Lead, first 30 seconds: "There are two kinds of Ozempic patients. The ones who suffer through the side effects. And the ones who quietly take one capsule a day and skip most of them. Here's how you join the second group."

Big idea: Identity, not pain. Belonging to the informed-patient tribe. Akkermansia is the password.

Pacing: 6 to 8 minutes. Drops pain-agitation almost entirely. Front-loads identity and tribal language, mechanism takes a back seat to "this is what people like you are doing."

Why it ranks third for cold: Identity messaging requires that the viewer already accepts the buying criterion (Akkermansia matters). Cold viewers don't. This VSL converts retargeted and lookalike audiences better than it converts pure cold, so deploy it in month 2 as the warm-audience VSL while ranks 1 and 2 fight for cold spend.

Pills: Identity Tribe Lv 5 Warm only

What this means for the first 8 weeks of spend

Weeks 1 to 2

  • 7 ad sets (the table above), all Phase 1 Linda
  • 14 creatives from the matrix, weighted to rows 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 9, 11
  • One VSL (rank 1 or rank 2, pick before launch)
  • Headlines from sophistication Lv 3 and Lv 4 only
  • Kill any ad below 0.8% CTR by day 3 (Funkmedia kill-day-3 rule)

Weeks 3 to 4

  • Refresh creative with rows 4, 7, 8, 10 (mechanism deepening)
  • Introduce second VSL as A/B against the first
  • Start Phase 3 staging ad set (row 14) at 5% of budget
  • Launch first retargeting layer with Lv 1 and Lv 2 headlines

Weeks 5 to 6

  • Add Lv 5 identity creatives (rows 7, 12) to fatigue swap
  • Spin Phase 2 ad set (Karen, considering) at 10%
  • Deploy VSL rank 3 to retargeting and lookalikes
  • First lookalike from purchaser list (1%)

Weeks 7 to 8

  • Scale winners 20% to 30% per increment, 3-day wait between
  • Add risk-reversal and proof creatives for skeptic retargeting
  • Phase 3 (Patricia, cycle-off) gets its own ad set at 10%
  • Refresh VSL with updated testimonials harvested from 30-day customers
