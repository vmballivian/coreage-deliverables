1. Audience for weeks 1 to 4

Phase 1 only. Women 48 to 62, US, household income $75k+, currently on Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy, or Zepbound for 4+ weeks. Solution-aware: she knows GLP-1 works, she's living the side effects, she's actively googling fixes. The pain is loud and the language is googlable (sulphur burps, "haven't gone in five days", muscle loss, GLP-1 fog). Identity move is "I'm doing this seriously, I want to do it right." Linda persona, full backstory, reddit verbatim quotes, and Schwartz state mapping all in persona v2. We are not redoing that work here.

Phase 2, 3, 4 are reserved. Pre-GLP-1 anxious (Phase 2), cycling off (Phase 3), and natural-leaning cold (Phase 4) are explicitly out of scope for this 4-week test. We open them once Phase 1 has a stable winning creative and predictable CPA. Wilson's instinct on this is correct: solution-aware first, expand later.

  • Detailed targeting: Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, semaglutide, tirzepatide, GLP-1, weight management, bariatric surgery, type 2 diabetes (broad layer)

6. Kill rules and scale rules

Kill rules

  • CTR below 0.7% on day 3 with $300+ spent on that creative, kill.
  • CPA above $120 by day 4 with no purchase trend, kill.
  • Frequency above 3.0 with CTR dropping 30%+ vs day 1 to 2, kill (audience fatigue).
  • Any creative flagged by Meta for policy review twice, replace.
  • If a whole LP variant (v1 or v2) underperforms the other by 40%+ on CVR with 500+ sessions, pause that variant week 3.
  • If Meta rejects the advertorial framing repeatedly, pivot to direct-to-product LP for that creative.

Scale rules

  • ROAS 2.0+ AND CTR 1.5%+ by day 5, mark "winner candidate", queue 2 iteration variants.
  • ROAS 2.5+ for 3 consecutive days, raise daily budget 20 to 30%.
  • ROAS 3.0+ for 5 consecutive days, duplicate creative into a broad/no-detail ad set.
  • If both v1 and v2 winners hold ROAS 3+, push the better one to 50%+ of week-4 budget.
  • If a single symptom wins across multiple creatives (e.g. sulphur burps wins twice), build 3 more around that symptom for week 3.
